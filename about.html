Our Story

Phakalane Plate & Bean was born from a simple belief: that the flavours of Botswana deserve a stage as elegant as any in the world.

Founded by a proud Motswana chef and entrepreneur, we set out to create a space where traditional dishes like seswaa and pap are served alongside modern café favourites — all in a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

We source our ingredients from local farmers and producers wherever possible, ensuring that every plate supports our community. Our coffee beans are roasted locally, and our menu changes with the seasons.

Whether you're here for a quiet morning coffee, a business lunch, or a family celebration, we welcome you like family.
